FlashAlign software that Aevonix owns or has authority to commercially license
is available under the GNU Affero General
Public License, version 3 only, or under a separate commercial agreement with
**Aevonix Inc**.

AGPL users may use FlashAlign commercially, charge for services and make
modifications, provided they meet the license's requirements. A commercial
license is an alternative for customers who need different terms, including
closed-source integration or modifications they do not wish to offer under
AGPL.

Contact **[contact@aevonix.com](mailto:contact@aevonix.com)**. Tell us how you plan
to deploy FlashAlign, whether you will distribute it to customers, and the
number of deployments or GPU nodes involved.

## What an agreement can cover

- Internal production deployments and customer-facing hosted services.
- Integration into a proprietary application.
- OEM distribution or embedding, when included in the order.
- Private modifications to the covered software, including contributions
  commercially licensed to Aevonix under its CLA.
- Support, response targets, maintenance, early access, warranties or
  indemnification, when expressly included in the order.

Pricing and scope are agreed in writing. There is no public price schedule or
automatic support entitlement. Aevonix may quote an annual company license,
deployment or GPU-node license, or an OEM agreement.

The [commercial license form](legal/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) and
[order form](legal/COMMERCIAL-ORDER.md) describe the proposed terms. They grant
no rights until both parties execute a completed order. Sending an inquiry,
downloading the software or paying an unaccepted quote does not create a
commercial license.

## Scope and third-party material

A commercial agreement covers only the software, versions and rights identified
in its order. It does not waive the licenses of inference engines, dependencies,
model weights or separately licensed profile artifacts. These retain their
own notices and conditions. See [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

AGPL's network-source requirement applies to the modified covered program.
Whether other code forms part of that covered work depends on how it is
combined with FlashAlign; merely calling an API does not, by itself, settle
that question. See [licensing guidance](legal/LICENSING.md) and obtain advice for
your particular integration if needed.

The commercial option does not change the rights already granted to recipients
under AGPL. It does not grant rights to use Aevonix or FlashAlign trademarks
except as expressly agreed. See [trademark use](TRADEMARKS.md).
