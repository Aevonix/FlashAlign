## Using the AGPL version

You may run FlashAlign, study it, modify it and use it commercially. AGPL does
not prohibit charging for services or software. Its conditions apply when you
convey covered copies and, under section 13, when users interact remotely over
a network with your modified version.

If you modify the covered program and let users interact with that version over
a network, offer those users the Corresponding Source for your version through
a prominent, no-charge network link. Include your relevant modifications and
the build and installation material required by the license. A link to an
unmodified upstream repository does not provide changes you made privately.

If you distribute covered software, retain the required notices and provide
the Corresponding Source as the license requires. An unmodified installation
does not become a privately owned version merely because you host it.

Independent programs do not automatically become AGPL software because they
share a machine or call an API. Whether a particular integration forms one
covered work depends on the integration. The license does not support a blanket
claim that every surrounding application must be public, or that every plugin
is independent.

## Choosing a commercial license

A signed commercial agreement can permit closed-source modifications,
proprietary integration, hosted services or OEM distribution within its agreed
scope. AGPL already permits commercial activity; the separate agreement supplies
different terms for the rights Aevonix is authorized to license.

The [commercial terms](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) and
[order form](COMMERCIAL-ORDER.md) are forms for an agreement, not a license
automatically granted by downloading this repository. Terms, quantities, fees,
governing law and any support commitments must be completed and executed.

## Contributions and third-party rights

Contributors retain their copyright and accept the [contribution terms](../CLA.md)
when submitting material. No separate signed form is required. The terms grant
Aevonix broad rights to use, modify, commercially license, sublicense and
transfer their contributions, including in closed-source client services.
There is no further contributor approval or royalty requirement for those
licensed uses. The CLA cannot convey rights the contributor does not control.

Model weights, starter profile artifacts, upstream inference engines, fonts and
other third-party material keep their own terms. Neither AGPL nor an Aevonix
commercial license waives those terms. Check the
[third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and the notices beside each
artifact. NVIDIA drivers and CUDA libraries are separately installed under
NVIDIA's licenses; they are not included in the FlashAlign source distribution.

The FlashAlign and Aevonix names and logos follow the
[trademark policy](../TRADEMARKS.md). No trademark registration is claimed here.
